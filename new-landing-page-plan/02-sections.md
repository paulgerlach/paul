## 2.2 End states (server, no JS, reduced motion)

- The hero's letter with the signature drawn and both badges shown.
- The preview with today's date and every placeholder; the three default clauses' sentences.
- "Zum nächstmöglichen Zeitpunkt" chosen, the date field absent.
- The checklist unticked, "0 von 4 geprüft", the bar empty.
- The download and copy buttons hidden without JS (`@media (scripting: none)`); the status line empty.
- Every `.rv` block shown (the reveal only hides blocks below the fold, in the browser).
- With reduced motion: no float, no drawing, no badge pop, no confetti, no hover movement (the landing reset turns off transitions and animations; `confettiFrom` checks).

Overflow checks at 320 px: the H1 (36 px there through `#hs-css`; "Messdienst kündigen." is ~330 px at 36 px and must wrap or hyphenate: the design's `overflow-wrap: break-word; hyphens: auto` under 620 px), the CTAs (stacked), the sheets (240 px), the fieldsets' inputs (`min-width: 0`), the switch (one column), the preview (26/22 px padding, long words in user input: `overflow-wrap: anywhere`), the three buttons, the checklist cards, the dark card's steps, the closing form.
