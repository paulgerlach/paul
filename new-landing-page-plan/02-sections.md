# Phase 2: Sections

Static markup and CSS of every section, ported from `#uv-css` (including its "Segment-Erweiterungen" block, which overrides the hero) and `#md-css`, with the `uv-`/`md-` prefixes dropped where the component's scope makes the class unique. Icons through `components/Icon.svelte` (phase 1.4). Every revealable element keeps the design's `rv` class for phase 3.

**Every animated block renders its end state on the server**: the portal entries show their "after" status, the statement is fully white, the calendar is ticked up to September with October in progress, the phone shows its notification and bars, the statement tile is filled with its check line, the before/after card is on "Mit Heidi", the rotator shows its first phrase, the numbers show their values, the mini dashboard is on "Monatlich" with every status row green and the toast shown, the big photo is at scale 1. So no-JS visitors and reduced motion get a complete page, and phase 3 only adds motion on top.

Layout basics from the design: `.dm-in` is `max-width: 1240px` with `padding-inline: clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px)`, the landing `.wrap` (check the padding and use `.wrap`). Unlike the light pages, sections have no hairlines and no `.alt` bands; spacing is per section (below). The buttons are the design's `.uv-btn` (10 px radius, 13×18 px, 14 px uppercase, 0.06em; `w` white → lime on hover, `d` `--card2`; 12×15 px / 13 px below 620), defined once in the page wrapper (`.uv :global(.uv-btn)`), since hero, rows and the Verwalter card share them.

Notes:

- Headings: one `<h1>`; `<h2>` for the four rows, Zahlen, the two audience cards, the quotes, the FAQ and the closing card; no H3 (the portal greeting is a `<p>`). `Statement`, `Rotator` and `Picture` have no heading in the design: `aria-label` on their `<section>`, as designed.
- Figures use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (bento, tile values, before/after sum), so a counting number doesn't change width.
- The tiles and mock-ups are light on the dark page: each sets `color: var(--ink)` on its root, so nothing inherits the page's white.
- Check at 375 px: the hero word (no overflow, the design's `nowrap`), the stacked CTAs, the middle panel alone with the side nav hidden and the status pills not overlapping the titles, the statement size, each tile at `min-height: 440px` (the phone pushed down, the calendar's note pill not wider than the tile, the statement tile's `104px` label column with "Durchschnittsnutzer"), the rotator's longest phrase ("Vergleich zum Durchschnitt") on two lines without overlap, the bento in one column with the mini dashboard scaled to 0.6, the audience cards, the closing card with the form, the floating CTA's short label.
