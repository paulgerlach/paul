## 2.2 End states (server, no JS, reduced motion)

- The preview: five ticks, "8 / 8" shown.
- Step 1: no pick, "0 / 8", the empty bar, Heidi's bar full, the verdict "Kreuzen Sie links an, was Ihr Anbieter heute leistet.", no list. Without JS: the toggles, the "Ihr Anbieter" row and the verdict hidden.
- Step 2: the start values and their result (89,00 €; 40,00 € / 24,00 € / 25,00 €; 10.680 €; 64.080 €), the stack at its shares.
- Every `.rv` block shown (the reveal only hides blocks below the fold, in the browser).
- With reduced motion: no tick pop, fade, bar growth, count or entrance (the landing reset turns off transitions and animations; `enterView` and `tweenNumber` check).

Overflow checks at 320 px: the H1 (36 px there through `#hs-css`), the CTAs (one column), the preview (70 px columns), the criteria rows (the toggle on its own row), the score card, the fields (one column), the result card's figure (wrapping its unit), the totals (one column), the tooltip (wrapping), the questions, the band and its figures (no-wrap values at 4.3em), the closing form.
