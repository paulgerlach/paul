# Phase 2: Sections

Static markup and CSS of every section, ported from `#dash-css` (plus `#cta-fix` for the closing chart's halo, the account menu and the Leckage-Wächter), with the `db-`/`hd-` prefixes dropped where the component's scope makes the class unique. Icons through `components/Icon.svelte` (phase 1.6). Every revealable element keeps the design's `rv` class for phase 3.

**Every animated block renders its end state on the server**: the hero chips' popovers hidden (hover only), the preview on its Dashboard view with every chart drawn, the KPI bars at full height with the totals ("12,48", "11,3"), the scroll scene as a plain list (README, Scroll scene), the bento demos in their resting state (two objects in the list, the notification stack, the leak chart complete with the badge, the billing card "Bereit", the share list with "Sie" and the first invitee), the hand-over with its three queued cards and four received rows at "14", the recommendation list checked ("7 Liegenschaften geprüft", counts 7 / 2 / 3 / 2), the marquee's rows in place, the closing chart drawn. So no-JS visitors and reduced motion get a complete page, and phase 3 only adds motion and interaction on top.

Layout basics from the design: `.db-in` is `max-width: 1240px` with `padding-inline: clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px)`: the landing `.wrap` (check the padding and use `.wrap`). Sections sit on `--bg` with a 1 px `--hair` line between them. Buttons are the design's `.db-btn` (8 px radius, 14×22 px, 16 px, weight 500; `w` white with a hairline, `k` ink), defined once in the page wrapper (`.db :global(.db-btn)`), since hero, integration and roadmap share them.

## 2.1 The preview's markup in detail

- **Charts** (`charts/`): a wrapper `div` (the design's `.hd-ch`, `position: relative`) holding the y labels (HTML, left column of 36–40 px as the design's padding), the plot area, and the x labels. In the plot area: an `<svg viewBox="0 0 100 100" preserveAspectRatio="none">` with gridlines (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), the area (gradient fill, id unique per chart: `$props.id()`), the previous-year line (dashed, 0.7 opacity) and the line (2.2 px). `BarChart` draws its bars as HTML (`<span style:height>` in a flex row, 4 px radius, the track variant with `#E4E8F2` tracks and the month labels on top instead of axes). `ForecastChart`: the band (polygon), the actual line, the dotted forecast, the dashed "today" line and its "HEUTE" pill (HTML). Ticks from `niceScale`, labels from `formatNumber`. The tooltip (`ChartTip`) and the hover marker are HTML, absent until a pointer moves (phase 3A.3).
- **Status** (`StatusList`): four buttons `.hd-s ok` (type icon, green check, "{n} {type}" bold, "In Ordnung · heute übertragen" in green).
- **Per object** (`PerObject`): rows of name, two stacked bars (`width` = value / 90), the sum with one decimal.
- **CO₂**: the figure with one decimal, "t CO₂", the sub line.
- **Data view**: when on, every chart is replaced by `DataTable` (`<table>` with "Monat", the year and "Vorjahr" columns, `caption` = the tile title, visually hidden), as the design's `.hd-dt`, scrolling inside the tile (max-height 190).
