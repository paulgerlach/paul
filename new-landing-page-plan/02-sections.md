## 2.2 End states (server, no JS, reduced motion)

- The card: the upload pane with the empty drop zone, the privacy note and "Beispiel ansehen" (the card hidden without JS); no flow, no result, no letter section.
- The scan: finished (four rows marked, four findings, "4 gefunden", the full bar, "4 Auffälligkeiten in dieser Abrechnung", the footer).
- The figures: "Verbraucherzentralen", 67, 67 dots, the text and source; "60 %" and "565 €".
- The errors: all eleven. The timeline: the bar full.
- Every `.rv` block shown (the reveal only hides blocks below the fold, in the browser).
- With reduced motion: no entrance, beam, count, dot stagger, bar growth or confetti (the landing reset turns off transitions and animations; `loopInView`, `enterView`, `countUp`, `tweenNumber` and `confettiFrom` check).
