## 2.2 End states (server, no JS, reduced motion)

- The scale: "Verbrauchsausweis" chosen, the marker at 51.1 % with "118", class D current, the others dimmed.
- The check: question 1 of 4, the bar empty, no "Zurück" (the card hidden without JS, `@media (scripting: none)`).
- The offers: "Alle", the four cards in full colour (the filter hidden without JS).
- The bars grown, the 36-month box shown.
- Every `.rv` block shown (the reveal only hides blocks below the fold, in the browser).
- With reduced motion: no slide, no count, no question entrance, no growth, no confetti, no hover movement (the landing reset turns off transitions and animations; `tweenNumber` and `confettiFrom` check).

Overflow checks at 320 px: the H1 (36 px there through `#hs-css`; "Liegenschaft." and "Energieausweis" are long at 36 px: the design's `overflow-wrap: break-word; hyphens: auto` under 620 px), the CTAs (stacked), the scale's nine cells (about 28 px each at 320 px; "A+" in 11 px fits), the marker (centred at 51 %, about 90 px wide), the two type buttons (`min-width: 0`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, the design's last rule), the answers, the result's actions, the offers' filter (three buttons, about 290 px: it wraps under the head), the table (two columns), the bars, the closing form.
