## 2.3 End states (server, no JS, reduced motion)

- The tool: the empty drop zone, the example button (hidden without JS), no list, no action row, the note.
- The result: not rendered until a run.
- The steps: every chip shown, "30.09." and "72", no card lit.
- The list: every row checked with its chip, the counts, "7 Liegenschaften geprüft".
- Every `.rv` block shown (the reveal only hides blocks below the fold, in the browser).
- With reduced motion: no entrance, scan line, spinner, loop, count or confetti; the run's waits are 0 ms (the landing reset turns off transitions and animations; `loopInView`, `enterView` and `confettiFrom` check).

Overflow checks at 320 px: the H1 (36 px there through `#hs-css`; "Vertragsunterlagen" is long at 36 px: the design's `overflow-wrap: break-word; hyphens: auto` under 620 px), the drop zone's line, the file rows (the name ellipsed, four dots and the ×), the action row, the KPIs (two columns), the overview rows (one column, the popover across the row), the bottom buttons (stretched), the documents and fields (one column), the list's filters (scrolling sideways, as the design), the search field, the decision cards, the closing form.
