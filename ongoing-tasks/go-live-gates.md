# Go-live gates of the new landing pages

Every page is indexed and in the sitemap after the cutover (decision of 2026-10-17). So every gate below must be done before the cutover PR (#440) merges; the `noindex` the new pages carry today only lasts until then. At the cutover each page loses `noindex` and gets its sitemap entry (cities: `live: true` in `cities/index.ts`).

`CLAUDE.md` and the gates refer to each other by these section numbers (§1 … §43). A gate's "its §4.1", "plan (…, §4.4)", "its open question" or "README" means the page's own plan, which is in git history at the commit named in the gate (and in that page's `CLAUDE.md` paragraph). A new page's gate goes in at the top as §2; renumber the others and the references in `CLAUDE.md` in the same commit.

The quotes, the customer logos and the security block (`TrustSeals`) are shared, so their items are settled once (§42) and the other gates point there.
